# Store Help Center & Policies

## Order Placement & Modifications
- **Order Confirmation:** An email and SMS with your Order ID (e.g., ORD1001) is sent immediately after placing an order.
- **Modifications & Cancellations:** Orders can only be modified or canceled within 30 minutes of placement. After 30 minutes, orders proceed to automated warehouse packaging and cannot be canceled or altered.
- **Change Delivery Address:** You can change your delivery address within 30 minutes of placing the order by contacting support. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed.

## Shipping & Delivery
- **Delivery Timeline:** Standard shipping takes 2 to 4 business days for major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi) and 3 to 6 business days for other regional areas nationwide.
- **Coverage Areas:** We deliver to all cities, towns, and postal codes across Pakistan.
- **Shipping Rates & Free Shipping:** Standard delivery costs PKR 200 nationwide. Orders over PKR 2,500 qualify for free standard shipping.
- **Courier Partners:** Dispatches are handled via registered couriers: TCS, Leopard Courier, and Trax.
- **International Shipping:** We currently only ship domestically within Pakistan. International delivery is not supported.

## Returns & Exchanges
- **Return Period:** You can request a return or exchange within 30 days of parcel delivery.
- **Condition Requirements:** Items must be unused, unwashed, unworn, in original packaging, and with all tags attached. Products with opened or torn retail packaging cannot be accepted.
- **Excluded Items:** Undergarments, socks, and final-sale clearance items cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Return Process:** To initiate a return, contact our support team with your Order ID and photos of the item. A courier pickup will be arranged.

## Refunds & Processing Times
- **Inspection Period:** Once the returned parcel arrives at our warehouse, quality inspection takes 2 to 3 business days.
- **Refund Timeline:** Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days to your original payment method.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds:** COD refunds are issued via direct bank transfer, EasyPaisa, or JazzCash upon providing verified account details.
- **Shipping Fees:** Original shipping charges (PKR 200) are non-refundable unless the return was caused by store error.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
- **Reporting Period:** If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, report it within 48 hours of delivery.
- **Resolution:** We provide a free immediate replacement or full refund (including shipping) upon receiving photo or video verification of the defect.

## Payment Methods & Security
- **Supported Payment Methods:** We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Visa, MasterCard, UnionPay, and mobile wallets (JazzCash and EasyPaisa).
- **Payment Security:** All online card transactions are processed through encrypted 3D-Secure payment gateways. Your card data is never stored on our servers.
- **Failed Payments:** If your payment fails, check your account balance and international/online transaction activation with your bank, or choose Cash on Delivery at checkout.

## Discounts & Promo Codes
- **First-Time Customer Discounts:** New customers receive a 10% discount on their first purchase by subscribing to our newsletter or using code WELCOME10 at checkout.
- **Applying Promo Codes:** Enter your discount or promo code in the 'Coupon Code' box on the checkout page before confirming payment. Codes cannot be applied retroactively.

## Customer Support Contact
